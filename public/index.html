Orderbook Lab

A Level 3 order book: every order is a separate order, waiting its turn in the queue at its price. Buyers and sellers arrive, change their minds and trade, continuously. Everything you see is invented in your browser - no real market data.

  • Above the line sellers, cheapest offer nearest the middle
  • Below the line buyers, highest bid nearest the middle
  • Each row one price: the volume resting there, and how many orders that is
  • Each bar that level's size against the biggest one on screen
